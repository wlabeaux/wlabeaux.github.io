THE Producer

I'm Will — a content producer and strategist with 3+ years of hands-on experience in video production, social-first editing, and live sports coverage. I've worked across the NFL and NCAA, producing content that has reached over 140 million impressions. I own the full pipeline — from concept and camera to edit and delivery — and I thrive where the stakes are highest.

I got my start producing digital content for CU Boulder before joining Colorado Football and Athletics, where I contributed to some of the most viral content in college sports during the Coach Prime era. Today I'm simultaneously working as a Live Content Correspondent for the NFL at Seattle Seahawks home games and as a Content Specialist at Learfield/UW Athletics, driving fan engagement across athletic social channels.

Outside of work, I love to ski and hike — anything outside, really.
