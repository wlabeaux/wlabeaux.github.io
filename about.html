ABOUT Me

Hi! I'm Will, a creative with hands-on experience in sports video production, editing, and social strategy. I've worked in the NFL and NCAA, creating and producing content with over 170 million combined impressions. I own the full production pipeline from pre-pro to camera to edit and delivery — I thrive when the stakes are high.

I started outside of sports developing skills and a passion for photography and videography. Then I got my start in Athletics at the University of Colorado, where I contributed to some of the most viral content in sports during the Coach Prime era at CU Football, including Travis Hunter's Heisman Campaign and NFL Draft coverage, among many other recaps and social pieces. Today I work for LEARFIELD at University of Washington as a Social Content Specialist creating sponsored content for Athletic social channels. I simultaneously work as a Live Content Correspondent for the NFL, shooting game days and other events in Seattle.

Outside of work I love to ski, golf, and hike — anything outside!
