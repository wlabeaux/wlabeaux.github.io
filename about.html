THE Producer

I'm Will — a content producer who thrives where the pressure is highest. I got my start managing social content for the CU Boulder Police Department before joining Colorado Football and Athletics, where I rose to Senior Creative Intern and helped produce some of the most viral content in college sports — including the Travis Hunter Heisman Campaign and NFL Draft coverage.

Today I'm working simultaneously as a Live Content Correspondent for the NFL covering Seattle Seahawks home games and as a Social Content Specialist at Learfield/UW Athletics. I own the full production pipeline — from ideation and camera operation to editing, motion graphics, and delivery.

I've mentored creative interns, led on-set production for media days and recruiting visits, and built content calendars that drive real audience growth. I work fast, stay calm under pressure, and never miss a deadline.
