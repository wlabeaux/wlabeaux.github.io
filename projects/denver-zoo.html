The Challenge

The Denver Zoo's existing website struggled with navigation complexity, outdated visual design, and a poor mobile experience — creating friction for visitors trying to plan a trip or find basic information.

The redesign focused on streamlining the visitor journey, bringing warmth and life to the visual experience, and creating a design system that scales across the full site.

My Approach

Starting with a heuristic evaluation and user flow analysis, I identified key drop-off points and confusing navigation patterns. From there I developed a new information architecture and high-fidelity design explorations that leaned into nature-forward visual language — rich photography, organic shapes, and a warmer color palette.
