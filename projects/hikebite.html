The Project

HikeBite is an iOS app designed to help hikers plan nutrition for their trails — solving a real pain point for outdoor enthusiasts who struggle to figure out how much food to bring and when to eat on the trail.

The challenge was making calorie and nutrition planning feel effortless and visual — not like a spreadsheet. The final design prioritizes trail-based thinking (elevation, duration, intensity) over abstract numbers.

My Process

Starting with user research and competitive analysis, I moved through wireframing and iterative prototyping. The visual direction leaned into organic, nature-inspired tones while staying clean enough to read on a sun-lit screen.
