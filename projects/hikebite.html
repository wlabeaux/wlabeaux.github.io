HikeBite is an iOS app created from the ground up from design to code. HikeBite is an easy way for backpackers and campers to tackle the toughest part of trip preparation: meal planning. Our app helps nature lovers curate personalized meal plans, ensuring they have the right amount of delicious food for their next adventure!
