The Role

As Senior Creative Intern for Colorado Football and Athletics, I planned, produced, and edited hundreds of high-impact video, photo, and social content pieces — contributing to content that generated over 60 million views and 4.6 million likes across CU's athletics channels.

I led on-set production for recruiting visits and media days, mentored and managed 3 creative interns, and was a key contributor on some of the program's most viral campaigns — including the Travis Hunter Heisman Campaign, NFL Draft Coverage, and multiple recruiting announcements.

The Work

From sideline game-day coverage to studio shoots and fast-turnaround social edits, the role demanded adaptability, speed, and creative instincts across every format. The content had to compete with national sports media — and consistently did.
